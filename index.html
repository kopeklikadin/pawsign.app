Why Paw Sign?

Designed with deaf dogs in mind

Created with deaf dogs and dogs with hearing difficulties in mind, Paw Sign is also useful for every dog parent who wants to build clearer daily routines through hand signs, body language, and organized care tracking.

Visual Communication Guide

Explore simple hand sign and visual command practices designed to support clearer communication with your dog.

Health Records

Keep vaccination records, medication notes, parasite care, observation logs, and important health files organized in one place.

Care Calendar

Track nutrition, water, weight, bath days, grooming, nails, oral care, eye care, ear care, and skin checks.

Reminders with Paw Sign Pro

Create reminders for care and health routines such as vaccination time, medication, bath days, and other important records.

Digital Health Archive

Save photos, documents, PDFs, health cards, checkup files, and veterinarian notes inside your dog’s profile.

Easy Sharing

Share prepared summary information with your veterinarian or caregivers via WhatsApp or email when needed.

Designed with deaf dogs in mind

Paw Sign was created from the need to support clearer visual communication with dogs who experience hearing difficulties.

The app includes hand sign and visual command practice guides. This section does not replace professional training; it is designed as a practical helper to make daily repetitions more organized and easier to follow.

Hand sign practices for basic commands

Who is Paw Sign for?

Paw Sign is designed for dog parents who want to:

Keep health and care records organized
Track vaccination, medication, parasite care, water, weight, and grooming
Support visual communication with a deaf dog or a dog with hearing difficulties
Build daily routines with hand signs and short visual command practices
Keep important files and notes easy to find
Share dog care information when needed
Important Note

Paw Sign is a record-keeping, tracking, and practical guide app. It does not provide veterinary advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency health guidance, or professional dog training services. For health-related concerns, please consult your veterinarian. For specific behavior or training situations, please consult qualified professionals.
